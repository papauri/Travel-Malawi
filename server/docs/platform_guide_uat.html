Platform Standards & QA

TRAVEL MALAWI — PLATFORM GUIDE & UAT MANUAL

Quality assurance protocols, verification checklists, and end-to-end user acceptance standards.

1. Purpose & Overview

This manual guides internal QA, operations, and partner support teams in verifying the integrity of the Travel Malawi booking platform across desktop, tablet, and mobile environments.

2. Core System Pillars

  • 0% Commission Direct Booking: Direct host-to-guest financial settlement without third-party deductions.
  • Local Rails: Seamless integration of Airtel Money, TNM Mpamba, and local bank transfers (MWK).
  • Native Offline Directions: Decimal/DMS GPS coordinates cached for seamless arrival on remote lakeside and park roads.

3. Acceptance Checklist

  • Discovery: Fast destination filtering across 9 primary regional tourism circuits.
  • Instant Inquiries: Pre-filled WhatsApp direct booking threads with real-time property verification.
  • Manager Storefront: Bulk room and rate modifications with inventory locking.
