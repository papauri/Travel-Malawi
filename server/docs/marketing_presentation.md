## 1. The problem: commission paid abroad

Malawian lodges and cottages pay **15% to 25% commission** on bookings made through foreign booking sites (Booking.com, Airbnb, Expedia), even for guests such as:

* An NGO or embassy employee living a few hours away in Lilongwe.
* A business traveller driving from Blantyre to Salima for a weekend conference.
* An international visitor who found the lodge on Instagram and wanted an easy way to pay.

**Example:** A 10-room lodge charging MK 90,000 a night at 50% occupancy sells about 1,825 room-nights a year, or about MK 164,250,000 in room revenue. Each night booked through a foreign site at 15–25% commission costs the lodge MK 13,500 to MK 22,500. If a quarter of those nights come through foreign sites, the lodge pays about **MK 6,159,000 to MK 10,266,000 a year** in commission.

**Positioning:** Travel Malawi is a direct booking platform that lets properties take bookings without paying that commission, with payment going straight to the property.

## 1. The problem: independent properties are hard to find

Many Malawian lodges and cottages are visible only through foreign listing sites, social media pages or word of mouth. Travellers often cannot find accurate photos, rates, directions or a working phone number, even for people such as:

* An NGO or embassy employee living a few hours away in Lilongwe.
* A business traveller driving from Blantyre to Salima for a weekend conference.
* An international visitor who found the lodge on Instagram and wanted accurate details.

**Positioning:** Travel Malawi is a free directory of independent properties. Each listing shows the property's own contact details, so travellers contact the property directly. Travel Malawi is an information directory only. Arrangements are made directly with the property.

## 2. Three main audiences

### Domestic and expatriate travellers (Lilongwe and Blantyre)

* **What they want:** Short-notice weekend trips to Lake Malawi, Zomba Plateau or Majete without long WhatsApp exchanges.
* **What frustrates them:** Cards declined on local machines, and foreign sites charging in USD with poor exchange rates.
* **Our message:** Book quickly and pay in Kwacha with Airtel Money, TNM Mpamba or bank transfer.

### International safari and wildlife travellers

* **What they want:** Direct contact with the property and confidence they will arrive without getting lost.
* **What worries them:** No mobile signal on remote roads (Liwonde, Nyika, Cape Maclear) and unverified properties.
* **Our message:** Maps and directions can be saved for offline use, and every host is verified.

### Property owners and general managers

* **What they care about:** They have invested heavily in their property and do not want to give a large share of revenue to foreign platforms.
* **What worries them:** Empty mid-week rooms, double bookings in busy periods, and complicated technology.
* **Our message:** 0% commission during launch, 2-way PMS calendar synchronization with RoomRaccoon and Airbnb, enquiries sent straight to WhatsApp, and dedicated concierge shadow mode to assist with setup.

## 4. Approach to property owners

We do not ask lodge owners to leave Booking.com. Asking them to give something up makes them less likely to say yes.

Instead, we position Travel Malawi as an addition:

> Keep Booking.com for the international guests it brings you. Use Travel Malawi for domestic and regional bookings at 0% commission, so you are not paying 20% on a guest driving from Blantyre.

Once owners see mobile money deposits arriving directly and have direct contact with guests, many will choose to put more of their availability on Travel Malawi.

## 5. 90-day launch plan

* **Days 1–30 (founding partners):** Sign up 40 founding properties across four hubs (Cape Maclear, Mangochi, Liwonde, Lilongwe), with a limit of 10 per hub.
* **Days 31–60 (corporate and expatriate travellers):** Partnerships with diplomatic missions and NGO offices in Lilongwe, and companies in Blantyre that run staff retreats.
* **Days 61–90 (high season):** National campaign around Easter, Lake of Stars and lake holidays.
