# Travel Malawi — Property Listing Master Reference

A comprehensive technical reference for creating and maintaining high-converting property profiles on Travel Malawi.

---

## 1. Property Categories
- **Lake & Beach Resorts / Cottages**: Properties situated along the shores of Lake Malawi (Cape Maclear, Mangochi, Salima, Nkhata Bay, Likoma).
- **Safari & Wildlife Camps**: Lodges situated inside or adjacent to national parks and wildlife reserves (Liwonde, Majete, Nyika, Kasungu, Nkhotakota).
- **Highlands & Mountain Escapes**: Lodges in elevated plateau regions (Mount Mulanje, Zomba Plateau, Dedza).
- **Urban & Boutique City Stays**: Hotels, guest houses, and business lodges in Lilongwe, Blantyre, and Mzuzu.

---

## 2. Location & Offline Navigation Guidelines

### Why Accurate GPS Coordinates Matter
Many travelers in Malawi navigate remote rural corridors with little or no cellular network service. The platform features an automated **Download Offline Map** system that pre-caches OpenStreetMap road tiles and satellite imagery across multi-tier zoom levels (zooms 9 through 16).

### Coordinates Setup Rules:
- **Pin Accuracy**: The map pin must indicate the actual vehicle access gate or reception area.
- **Coordinates Format**: Decimal degrees (e.g. `-14.0256, 34.9213`) or DMS coordinates.
- **Turn-Off Directions**: Detail the distance in kilometers from the nearest paved M-road (e.g. M1, M5 Lakeshore Road, M3).
- **Vehicle Requirements**: Clearly note if a high-clearance 4WD vehicle is required, particularly during rainy season months (December to April).

---

## 3. Dual-Currency Pricing Conventions
- **Malawian Kwacha (MWK)**: Round cleanly to the nearest 1,000 MWK (e.g. MK 120,000, not MK 119,842).
- **United States Dollar (USD)**: Whole numbers or rounded to the nearest $5 (e.g. $85 or $90).
- **Parity**: Both rates must remain fair and competitive without arbitrary exchange penalty markups.

---

## 4. Inquiries & Booking Settlement
- Direct WhatsApp connection pre-fills booking dates, guest counts, and unique property reference codes.
- Local mobile money details (Airtel Money and TNM Mpamba merchant or agent lines) can be provided directly to confirmed guests.
- Digital Stay Vouchers provide travelers with an offline QR code and check-in PIN upon reservation approval.
