# Operations and Field Playbook
*Partner outreach, common objections and listing quality checks*

## 1. Keep it easy for the property owner

Lodge owners and managers in Malawi are busy with generators, road repairs, staff and supplies. **Anything that feels like extra work is likely to be postponed.**

### The main rule for field outreach

**Do not ask a host to "sign up", "fill out a form" or "learn a system".** Do the work for them:

> Send us four photos and your current rates on WhatsApp. Our team will build your listing for free, usually within about eight minutes.

## 2. Three-message WhatsApp sequence

Use this sequence on WhatsApp or in person with lodge managers and cottage owners.

### Message 1: Introduction and question

> Muli bwanji [Manager name]. We have seen the lakefront cottages at [Property name] and they look lovely. Quick question: for your weekend guests from Lilongwe and Blantyre, are you still paying foreign booking websites 18% to 22% commission?

*Purpose:* Start a conversation and raise the cost of commission without being pushy.

### Message 2: Show a preview (24 hours later)

> We have put together a private preview of how [Property name] would look on Travel Malawi, with Airtel Money and TNM Mpamba deposits paid directly to you and 0% commission: [Preview link]. Guests message your WhatsApp directly. Would you like us to make it live?

*Purpose:* Show work already done, so the owner can see the result straight away.

### Message 3: Founding partner invitation (48 hours later)

> Takulandirani [Manager name]. We are confirming our founding partner group for [Region] this Friday, limited to 10 properties (0% commission for 90 days and free listing setup). There are 2 places left in your area and we wanted to offer you one first.

*Purpose:* Make clear that places in the founding group are limited.

## 4. Two-Way Direct Guest Chat & Ulendo Concierge AI Copilot

Make sure every prospective host understands our two flagship communication advantages:
1. **Direct Guest Chat (In-App & WhatsApp):** Guests can chat directly with property managers on every listing and booking. No hidden numbers, no delayed call center emails. Perfect for Malawian travel logistics (boat arrivals from Monkey Bay, dirt road navigation, Chambo dinner prep).
2. **Ulendo Concierge AI for Managers:** Inside the Host Dashboard, managers can chat 24/7 with Ulendo—our resident Malawian hospitality AI concierge—to recommend rates in MWK and USD, draft polite deposit reminders, update daily dining boards, and optimize amenities with 1 tap.

## 5. Listing quality checks

Before a property is marked **Approved** in the Admin Dashboard, check:

1. **Gate GPS coordinates.** The pin is at the vehicle entrance gate, not the district post office. Offline navigation depends on this.
2. **Real photos (at least 3).** Outside view, bedroom, and bathroom or view. No blurry or watermarked images.
3. **Prices in both currencies.** MWK rounded to the nearest 1,000 and USD in whole dollars or rounded to the nearest $5.
4. **Verified WhatsApp number.** The host replies to a test message within 15 minutes.
5. **Chat presence configured.** Verify the host knows how to access direct guest chats and Ulendo Concierge in their dashboard.
6. **2-Way Calendar Connection Test Verified.** Click "Test Connection" in the room settings. Verify that external iCal feeds (RoomRaccoon, Airbnb, NightsBridge) return valid RFC 5545 events with latency under 2,000ms.
7. **One-Click Listing Approval.** Admins and Marketing staff can directly approve or reject pending stays from the preview card or host dashboard banner.

## 6. Staff & Concierge "Shadow a Host" Mode SOP

Admins and Marketing team members can securely shadow any property manager's dashboard in real-time to troubleshoot listings, verify calendar links, and support hosts without requiring their passwords:

### How to Initiate a Shadow Session
1. **Open Shadow Menu:** Click **"Shadow a Manager"** in the top navigation bar (available to Admins and Marketing staff).
2. **Search Property or Host:** Use the instant search bar to find the lodge, cottage, or host by name or location.
3. **Launch Session:** Click **"Shadow Host"**. You will immediately see the platform from the host's exact perspective.
4. **Session Indicator:** A prominent amber banner displays across the top of the screen: *"Shadowing [Property Name] as Host"*.
5. **Exit Cleanly:** Click **"Exit Shadow Mode"** in the banner at any time to return immediately to your administrative view.

### Permitted Concierge Actions During Shadow Mode
* **PMS Feed Diagnostics:** Run the "Test Connection" tool on the host's RoomRaccoon or Airbnb iCal feed to inspect response times and ensure date-blocking is working smoothly.
* **Pricing & Room Configuration:** Help the host configure room inventory, seasonal pricing, and amenity tags.
* **Payment Rails Verification:** Double-check that the host's Airtel Money, TNM Mpamba, and local bank account details (National Bank, Standard Bank, FDH Bank, Centenary Bank) are formatted clearly for guest deposits.
* **Audit Trail:** Every shadow session and configuration update is cryptographically logged with your staff user ID and timestamp for security and compliance.
