# Travel Malawi — Operations & Growth Starter Pack

**Role**: Operations, Host Acquisition & Quality Assurance Team  
**Scope**: Field operations across Malawi, host onboarding, listing verification, and guest journey support.

---

## 1. The Four Operational Pillars

### Pillar 1: Property Acquisition & Onboarding
- Target independent lodges, lakeside cottages, boutique B&Bs, and safari camps across all major Malawian travel regions.
- Target corridors:
  - **Lake Malawi**: Cape Maclear, Mangochi, Senga Bay (Salima), Nkhata Bay, Likoma Island.
  - **Wildlife & Conservation**: Liwonde National Park, Majete Wildlife Reserve, Nyika Plateau, Kasungu, Nkhotakota.
  - **Scenic Mountains**: Mount Mulanje, Zomba Plateau, Dedza Mountain.
  - **Urban Hubs**: Lilongwe (Area 10, Area 43, City Centre, Old Town) and Blantyre (Nyambadwe, Sunnyside, Mandala).

### Pillar 2: Quality Control & GPS Precision
- Every property listing must meet rigorous quality standards:
  - Minimum 3 authentic, high-resolution photos (exterior, room, bathroom/dining).
  - Exact GPS latitude and longitude pin (crucial for the **Offline Map** download system).
  - Driving directions from the nearest paved highway or landmark, including 4WD advisories or bridge notes.
  - Accurate dual pricing in both MWK and USD with no hidden charges.
  - Verified contact information (direct phone, WhatsApp, and email).

### Pillar 3: Remote Travel Support & Offline Navigation
- Advise property managers on verifying their offline road directions:
  - Ensure coordinates match the lodge gate, not just the general district center.
  - Remind hosts that guests traveling without cellular data rely on the platform's **Download Offline Map** feature.
  - Ensure host emergency phone numbers are up to date so travelers can call via satellite or direct cellular once near the lodge.

### Pillar 4: Guest Concierge & Direct WhatsApp Facilitation
- Monitor incoming booking inquiries and support travelers planning multi-stop journeys across Malawi.
- Assist guests with travel tips on road conditions (e.g., M1 road updates, Lakeshore road conditions, seasonal rains).

---

## 2. Key Value Pitch for Host Acquisition

When approaching prospective hosts, emphasize:
1. **0% Commission**: "Keep every Kwacha and Dollar. No 15–25% cuts like Booking.com."
2. **Direct Guest WhatsApp**: "Talk directly to your guests, answer questions, and build lasting customer relationships."
3. **Local Payment Settlement**: "Receive deposit payments directly into your Airtel Money, TNM Mpamba, or National Bank of Malawi / Standard Bank account."
4. **Offline Map Resilience**: "Your guests can download your property's offline map and navigate remote dirt tracks even after cellular networks drop out."
