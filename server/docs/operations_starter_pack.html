Host acquisition scripts, objection disarming matrices, and zero-inertia conversion protocols.

1 The Core Axiom: Zero Cognitive Inertia

Lodge managers in Malawi are overwhelmed with daily logistics (generators, roads, supplies). Never ask them to "sign up" or "fill out a form." Simply say: "Send us 3 photos and your rate card on WhatsApp. We build your profile for you in 8 minutes for free."

2 The 3-Touch WhatsApp Sequence

Touch 1: The Sincere Compliment + Provocative Question

"Muli bwanji [Name]! Admiring your stunning chalets. Quick question: on weekend bookings from Lilongwe/Blantyre, are you still paying foreign websites 18%–22% commission?"

Goal: Open a conversational loop without being pushy.
Touch 2: The Zero-Inertia Value Demo (24 Hours Later)

"We built a sample preview of [Property] on Travel Malawi with direct Airtel/Mpamba payouts and 0% commission. Direct link: [Preview]. Would you like us to activate this?"

Goal: Trigger endowment effect (the work is already done).
Touch 3: The Velvet Rope / Scarcity Close (48 Hours Later)

"Takulandirani [Name] — locking our Founding Host Cohort for [Region] at 10 flagship stays this Friday (0% commission for 90 days). 2 slots left in your area; wanted to offer you first choice."

Goal: Status and loss aversion.

3 Disarming Critical Objections

The Objection The Psychology The Cunning Turnaround
