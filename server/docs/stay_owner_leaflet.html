What You Get as a Partner Property

A one-page summary for lodge, cottage and hotel owners

Keep more of what you earn

Tired of paying 15% to 25% of every booking to foreign travel websites that do not know Malawi?

Travel Malawi is a direct booking platform for independent properties in Malawi. It was designed with Malawian lodge managers, safari operators and cottage owners, and connects you directly with domestic travellers, international visitors, NGO staff and business travellers.

Five things you get from day one

1. Your own booking page

  • A fast, mobile-friendly page showing your rooms, rates in Kwacha and USD, amenities, menu and activities.
  • A photo gallery of your rooms.
  • Accurate GPS location and directions, so guests can find you on unpaved roads.

2. Payments to your own accounts

  • Guests pay deposits directly to your Airtel Money or TNM Mpamba account in Malawian Kwacha (MWK).
  • Bank transfers to National Bank of Malawi (NBM), Standard Bank, FDH Bank or Centenary Bank.
  • Visa and Mastercard, and cash on arrival if your policy allows it.
  • No foreign exchange losses and no waiting for international transfers.

3. Ulendo, the AI concierge

  • Ulendo (Chichewa for "journey") answers guest questions at any time of day.
  • It handles common questions such as "Do you have solar backup?", "Can we arrange a boat to Bird Island?" or "What is for dinner?"
  • It replies in English, and in Chichewa when a guest writes in Chichewa.
  • It takes booking requests and passes them to you.

4. Calendar and room availability

  • Live availability that prevents double bookings.
  • Block dates in one step when rooms are booked offline or reserved for groups.
  • Change rates for several rooms at once for busy weekends (Easter, Lake of Stars, Christmas).

5. Reviews and direct guest contact

  • Show your TripAdvisor and Google reviews on your listing.
  • Chat directly with guests, so returning travellers come straight back to you.

How to join (about 10 minutes)

  1. Send your details. Send four photos (bed, view, bathroom, dining), your room names and rates in MWK or USD, and your WhatsApp number.
  2. We build your page. Our team sets up your listing at no cost.
  3. Receive guests directly. Guests find you and pay you directly, and you keep 100% of the room rate during the launch period.

Founding partner offer

  • 0% commission for your first 90 days.
  • Free listing setup, including your menu.
  • Featured placement on the Travel Malawi homepage.

Places are limited to 10 founding properties per area, across Lake Malawi, Lilongwe, Blantyre and the national parks.
