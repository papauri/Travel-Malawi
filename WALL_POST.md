## 🌟 Primary Wall Post (Facebook / LinkedIn / Host Community Board)

When we set out to build **Travel Malawi**, our vision was simple yet deeply personal: to give independent lodge owners, cottage keepers, boutique guesthouses, and safari camps across the Warm Heart of Africa a digital home where they own their bookings, keep direct relationships with their guests, and get discovered on their own terms.

Technology only matters when real people trust it. Today, we want to pause and say a heartfelt **Zikomo kwambiri** to the incredible property owners and managers who took a leap with us and officially brought their stays onto the platform:

🌿 **Wonanji Esther Sande** — *Esthers Guest House - Boat house* (Lilongwe)
Wonanji has built a peaceful, immaculate 3-bedroom en-suite haven in Area 49 New Gulliver. Equipped with Starlink, backup solar power, and self-catering comforts, she's welcoming families, business travelers, and wedding parties to Lilongwe with genuine warmth.

🌊 **Felipe & the Crew** — *Soul Rebel Lodge & Backpackers* (Nkhata Bay)
Perched right above Chikale Beach on the northern shores of Lake Malawi, Felipe’s eco-friendly lodge is pure magic. From morning yoga overlooking turquoise waters to kayak rentals and cozy lakeside hammocks, Soul Rebel proves why Lake Malawi captures the hearts of travelers worldwide.

🏖️ **Pamela Msukwa** — *Rosalyn's Beach Hotel* (Cape Maclear, Mangochi)
Nestled right along the golden sands and calm waters of Chembe Village and Makawa, Pamela is treating visitors to stunning lake panoramas, fresh Chambo, and unforgettable sunsets.

### Why this milestone means so much
To our hosts: you aren't just "listings" in a database. You are the heartbeat of tourism in this country. Every time a traveler discovers your spot, books without painful intermediary fees, and gets a warm Malawian welcome at your door, that's what makes this journey worth every line of code.

We're learning with you, improving the platform alongside you every single day, and making sure Malawian hospitality shines on the world stage.

Thank you for trusting us with your life’s work. We are in this together, and we are just getting started. 🇲🇼✨

**Takulandirani. We grow together.**
